Funding & scholarships

Support for your investment.

OSB offers a portfolio of scholarships designed to recognize leadership, professional achievement, impact, and financial need across a range of executive profiles.

Flagship award
Executive Leadership & Merit

Dean's Leadership Scholarship

For senior executives demonstrating exceptional leadership, organizational impact, and strategic vision across industries.

Women in Leadership

Women in Leadership Scholarship

For women executives demonstrating leadership achievement and a commitment to advancing their impact in business and society.

Entrepreneurship & Family Business

Entrepreneurship & Family Enterprise Scholarship

For founders, entrepreneurs, SME owners, family-business leaders, and innovators driving growth and transformation.

Public Sector Leadership

Public Sector & Government Leadership Scholarship

For leaders serving in government, public institutions, municipalities, regulatory bodies, healthcare, education, and international organizations.

Need-Based

Executive Opportunity Scholarship

For high-potential, self-funded executives who have demonstrated exceptional professional achievement, leadership capability, and a strong commitment to advancing their careers despite financial constraints.

Alumni Excellence

Alumni Excellence Scholarship

For accomplished AUB alumni whose leadership experience, professional achievements, and diverse perspectives will strengthen and enrich the Executive MBA learning community.

Corporate Partnership

Corporate Partnership Scholarship

For high-performing executives nominated or sponsored by partner organizations committed to developing leadership talent and accelerating organizational success.

Scholarships are subject to eligibility criteria, availability, and program policies.
