Leadership capital that extends far beyond graduation.

The OSB Executive MBA at the American University of Beirut is designed for accomplished professionals seeking to lead with greater strategic vision, influence, and impact. Through immersive residencies, globally oriented learning, and a dedicated leadership development journey, participants strengthen their ability to navigate complexity, drive transformation, and create lasting value within their organizations.

Beyond the classroom, participants become part of a powerful executive ecosystem that brings together peers, faculty, industry leaders, and AUB's global alumni network. These relationships foster new perspectives, expand professional opportunities, and build the leadership capital needed to influence decisions, mobilize stakeholders, and lead confidently across industries and borders.
