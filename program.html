The program

Built around the realities of executive life.

The OSB Executive MBA is designed for experienced professionals who want to advance their leadership journey without stepping away from their careers. Delivered over 15 months through an immersive residency-based format, the program combines the intensity of executive learning with the flexibility required by working professionals.

Learning is grounded in real business challenges, enabling participants to apply new perspectives, frameworks, and insights directly within their organizations.

Global

International Residency

As part of the journey, participants engage in an International Residency at a partner global business school, expanding their exposure to international markets, business practices, and leadership perspectives.

Integrated

Capstone Project

Throughout the program, participants integrate their learning through a Capstone Project focused on a real organizational challenge, translating insights into actionable solutions and measurable business impact.
