The program

Built around the realities of executive life.

The OSB Executive MBA is built around the realities of executive life. Its residency-based format enables professionals to continue advancing their careers while engaging in a transformative learning journey grounded in real-world business application. Delivered approximately every six weeks over 15 months, the program balances flexibility with intensity, ensuring participants can apply learning immediately in their organizations.

Global

International Residency

As part of the journey, participants engage in an International Residency at a global partner business school, expanding their exposure to international markets, business practices, and leadership models.

The finale

Capstone Project

The program culminates in a capstone project that enables participants to tackle real organizational challenges and translate learning into measurable business outcomes.

The experience

A 15-month journey shaped by eight defining experiences, on campus in Beirut, abroad on the International Residency, and inside your own organization.

01

Immersive On-campus Residencies

Structured in-person residencies designed to foster focused executive learning, collaboration, and immediate business application.

02

International Residency

Gain global business exposure through an integrated residency at a partner business school, broadening your strategic and leadership perspective.

03

Leadership Development Program

Strengthen your executive presence, self-awareness, communication, and strategic influence through a dedicated leadership journey.

04

Future-Focused Curriculum

Engage with market-relevant topics including strategy, innovation, digital transformation, AI, and sustainable business growth.

05

World-Class Faculty

Learn from experienced faculty and thought leaders who combine academic excellence with practical business expertise.

06

Industry Engagement

Benefit from executive networking, guest speakers, and direct exposure to industry leaders and market insight.

07

Capstone Project

Translate learning into action by addressing real organizational and market challenges through a practical capstone experience.

08

Executive Network & Alumni Community

Build lifelong professional connections through a powerful ecosystem of peers, faculty, and the wider AUB alumni network.
