Eligibility

Who this program is for.

The Executive MBA is designed for established professionals with significant experience, demonstrated leadership responsibility, and the ambition to advance their impact at an organizational and strategic level.

Minimum 8 years of professional experience

Demonstrated leadership responsibilities

Undergraduate degree from an accredited institution

Strong career progression

Application requirements

What to prepare.

A complete application includes the following:

01

Online Application

Complete and submit the online application form.

02

Updated CV

A current resume outlining your professional and leadership experience.

03

University Degree

An undergraduate degree from a recognized institution.

04

Two Recommendation Letters

From individuals who can speak to your leadership experience and professional impact.

05

Personal Essays

Short essays outlining your goals, motivation, and aspirations for the Executive MBA.

06

Admissions Interview

A conversation with an admissions committee member to explore your experience, leadership journey, and goals.

Application process

From application to enrollment.

A clear, guided path from application to a confirmed place in the Executive MBA.

01

Submit Your Application

Complete the online application and upload all required documents.

02

Application Review

Your application is reviewed to assess your professional experience, career progression, leadership track record, academic background, and overall fit for the Executive MBA.

03

Admissions Interview

Eligible candidates are invited for an interview to discuss their professional journey, leadership experience, career goals, and motivation for pursuing the Executive MBA.

04

Committee Evaluation

Applications and interview outcomes are reviewed by the Admissions Committee to determine each candidate's overall fit for the program.

05

Admission Decision

Applicants are notified of the Admissions Committee's decision. Successful candidates receive an official offer of admission with details on the next steps toward enrollment.

06

Enrollment Confirmation

Admitted candidates secure their place in the cohort by submitting a non-refundable confirmation fee and completing the required enrollment steps.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early application is encouraged to secure your place and increase your chances for scholarship consideration.
