The OSB Executive MBA at the American University of Beirut is designed for experienced professionals ready to lead at a higher strategic level. Through immersive residencies, a global learning experience, and a dedicated leadership development journey, participants strengthen their ability to navigate complexity, drive transformation, and create measurable business impact. Beyond the academic experience, participants gain access to a powerful executive ecosystem of peers, faculty, industry leaders, and the wider AUB alumni network, building strategic relationships and leadership capital that extend far beyond graduation.
