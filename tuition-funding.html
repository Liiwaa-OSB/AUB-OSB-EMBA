Support for your investment.

OSB offers a portfolio of named, strategic scholarships, recognizing leadership, impact, and potential across a range of executive profiles.

Flagship award
Executive Leadership & Merit

Dean's Leadership Scholarship

For senior executives demonstrating exceptional leadership, organizational impact, and strategic vision across all industries.

Women in Leadership

Women in Leadership Scholarship

Women executives with strong leadership potential and a commitment to advancing women in business and society.

Entrepreneurship & Family Business

Entrepreneurship & Family Enterprise Scholarship

Founders, entrepreneurs, SME owners, family-business successors, innovation leaders, and business owners driving growth and transformation.

Public Sector Leadership

Public Sector & Government Leadership Scholarship

Leaders from government ministries, municipalities, regulatory authorities, public institutions, healthcare, education, and international organizations.

Need-Based

Executive Opportunity Scholarship

Outstanding self-funded executives with strong leadership potential and professional achievement who would benefit from financial assistance.

Alumni Excellence

Alumni Excellence Scholarship

AUB alumni demonstrating the professional achievement to enroll in and enrich the program.

Corporate Partnership

Corporate Partnership Scholarship

Employees nominated or sponsored by a company investing in their executive development.
