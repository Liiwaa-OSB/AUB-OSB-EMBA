Eligibility

Who this program is for.

The Executive MBA is built for established leaders who bring significant professional experience, a track record of leadership, and the ambition to create greater organizational impact. Candidates typically meet the following criteria.

Experience

Minimum 8 years of professional experience

Leadership

Demonstrated leadership responsibilities

Education

Recognized undergraduate degree

Progression

Strong career progression

Application requirements

What to prepare.

A complete application includes each of the following six items.

01

Online application

Complete the online application form.

02

Updated CV

A current resume outlining your professional experience.

03

University transcripts

Official records from your undergraduate degree.

04

Two recommendation letters

From referees who can speak to your leadership.

05

Personal essays

Short essays on your goals and motivation.

06

Admissions interview

A conversation with the admissions committee.

Application process

From application to enrollment.

A clear, guided path, six steps from first application to a confirmed seat.

01

Submit Your Application

Complete the online application and upload all required documents.

02

Application Review

Your application is carefully reviewed to assess professional experience, leadership potential, academic background, and overall fit for the program.

03

Admissions Interview

Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview to discuss their professional journey, leadership aspirations, and motivation for pursuing the Executive MBA.

04

Committee Evaluation

Applications and interview outcomes are reviewed by the Admissions Committee for final evaluation and decision.

05

Admission Decision

Applicants are notified of the committee's decision and, if admitted, receive an official offer of admission.

06

Enrollment Confirmation

To secure your seat in the cohort, admitted candidates confirm enrollment by submitting the non-refundable confirmation fee.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early application is encouraged to secure your place and maximize scholarship consideration. Final deadline to apply: August 31.
