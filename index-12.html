Leadership capital that extends far beyond graduation.

The OSB Executive MBA at the American University of Beirut is designed for experienced professionals ready to lead at a higher strategic level. Through immersive residencies, a global learning experience, and a dedicated leadership development journey, participants strengthen their ability to navigate complexity, drive transformation, and create measurable business impact.

Beyond the academic experience, participants gain access to a powerful executive ecosystem of peers, faculty, industry leaders, and the wider AUB alumni network, building strategic relationships and leadership capital that extend far beyond graduation.

Six pillars define the experience, and what it means for your leadership.

01

Leadership Development

A dedicated journey to strengthen executive presence, self-awareness, communication, and strategic influence.

02

Strategic Business Learning

An integrated business perspective across strategy, finance, operations, marketing, and leadership, enabling stronger decisions and immediate workplace application.

03

Applied Business Impact

Translate learning into actionable insight through real business challenges, case-based discussions, and practical frameworks you can apply directly.

04

Executive Network

Build strategic relationships with accomplished peers, faculty, and industry leaders that extend your professional influence and leadership.

05

International Residency

Gain global exposure through an immersive residency at a partner business school, broadening your view of international markets and leadership practice.

06

AUB Ecosystem & Alumni Network

Access AUB's academic ecosystem and lifelong alumni community, extending the value of your Executive MBA well beyond graduation.
